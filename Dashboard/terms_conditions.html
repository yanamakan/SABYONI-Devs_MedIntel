Section 04

User Roles & Permissions

The Platform operates on a strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) architecture. Each user is assigned one role that governs their permissions. Attempting to access features outside your assigned role is prohibited.

Patient

Register and manage personal health profiles; submit symptoms via AI assistant; view prescriptions, appointments, and notifications; upload supporting documents; receive digital prescriptions.

Doctor / Clinician

Access AI triage summaries; view full patient history and medical records; issue digital prescriptions; manage consultation notes; access clinical decision support features.

Secretary / Receptionist

Manage appointment scheduling; confirm urgent triage cases; allocate doctors to patient queues; send patient notifications; view operational dashboards.

System Administrator

Configure system settings; manage user accounts and access controls; review audit logs; ensure regulatory compliance; manage data backup and security configurations.

Super User (Medical Director)

Access system-wide analytics; override clinical and administrative decisions; approve AI model updates; oversee compliance; manage institutional policies.

Restrictions — All Users

No user may share credentials, impersonate another user, access data outside their role, export patient records without authorisation, or use the Platform for any non-healthcare commercial purpose.

Section 05

AI Features & Limitations

5.1 Nature of AI Triage

The AI Triage and Clinical Summary module is a decision support tool designed to assist healthcare professionals. It categorises patient-reported symptoms into urgency levels (low, medium, high, emergency) and generates structured pre-consultation reports.

Clinical Decision Support — Not a Diagnostic Tool

AI-generated triage reports and clinical summaries do not constitute medical diagnoses, clinical decisions, or treatment prescriptions. All clinical decisions remain the exclusive responsibility of licensed healthcare professionals.

5.2 Accuracy & Limitations

While the AI system is designed with clinical input, it may produce incomplete, inaccurate, or contextually inappropriate outputs. The Platform expressly disclaims any warranty regarding the clinical accuracy of AI-generated content. Healthcare professionals must independently verify all AI outputs before acting on them.

5.3 AI Model Updates

AI models are updated periodically to improve accuracy. Material updates that affect clinical workflows will require Super User approval and users will be notified of significant changes.

5.4 Patient Consent for AI Processing

By submitting symptoms through the AI assistant, patients consent to their symptom data being processed by the AI module for the purpose of generating triage reports accessible to treating healthcare professionals. This consent is withdrawable at any time.

All patient data is stored within the Republic of South Africa on encrypted cloud infrastructure. Any cross-border transfer will only occur to countries with equivalent data protection standards and will comply with POPIA section 72 requirements.

6.4 Data Breaches

In the event of a security breach, we will notify affected individuals and the Information Regulator within 72 hours of becoming aware, as required by POPIA.
